# Marketing Campaign Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze bank marketing campaign data to understand customer subscription patterns and identify the factors associated with higher subscription rates.

## 2. Dataset

The project uses the Bank Marketing dataset containing customer information and details about marketing campaign contacts.

* Original records: 41,188
* Duplicate records removed: 12
* Final records: 41,176
* Total columns: 21
* Missing values: None found

## 3. Work Completed

1. Loaded the dataset using Python and Pandas.
2. Checked the dataset structure, column names, and data types.
3. Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
4. Removed 12 duplicate records.
5. Analyzed customer demographics, including age, job, education, and marital status.
6. Analyzed campaign details, including contact method, number of contacts, month, and day of the week.
7. Examined previous campaign outcomes and their relationship with current subscriptions.
8. Calculated subscription rates and compared customer groups.
9. Created visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn.
10. Calculated the overall campaign conversion rate.

## 4. Key Findings

* The overall subscription rate was 11.27%.
* Cellular contacts had a subscription rate of 14.74%, compared with 5.23% for telephone contacts.
* Students had the highest subscription rate by job category at 31.43%, followed by retired customers at 25.26%.
* Customers whose previous campaign was successful had a current subscription rate of 65.11%.
* March had the highest monthly subscription rate at 50.55%, while May had the highest number of subscriptions at 886.
* Customers who subscribed had a longer average call duration than those who did not subscribe.
* Subscription rates varied across age groups, education levels, marital statuses, and campaign contact counts.

## 5. Conclusion

This project helped me understand how Python can be used to clean data, perform exploratory data analysis, calculate subscription rates, and create visualizations.

The analysis identified differences in subscription rates across customer groups, contact methods, previous campaign outcomes, and months.

These findings can help the bank investigate customer response patterns and improve future campaign planning. The results show associations in the dataset and do not establish that any individual factor directly causes a customer to subscribe.
